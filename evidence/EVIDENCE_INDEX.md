## Notes

The assigned Ubuntu practice environment did not provide functional auditd, journalctl logging, auth.log or syslog services.

Rather than fabricate evidence, all observed errors and environmental limitations were documented and evaluated as governance findings within the final audit report.

Primary findings include:

- W4-F01 Audit System Not Operating
- W4-F02 Log Management Deficiency
- W4-F03 Patch Backlog and Vulnerable Packages
- W4-F04 Hardening Gaps
- W4-F05 Privileged Access Monitoring Gap
- W4-F06 DNS Resilience Issue
- W4-F07 Platform Resource Limitation

Refer to the final report for complete screenshots, analysis, remediation actions and governance mapping.
